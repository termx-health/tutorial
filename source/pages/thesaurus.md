In TermX the **thesaurus** is the collaborative knowledge platform — the [wiki](page:wiki). It lets teams capture, organise and cross-link the documentation that surrounds a terminology: modelling decisions, editorial guidelines, project notes and end-user guides (this tutorial is itself published from a TermX wiki space).

## What the thesaurus provides

- **Pages** authored in a rich [markdown syntax](page:basic-markdown-syntax) with many [extensions](page:extended-markdown-syntax) for drawing, modelling and linking.
- **Navigation menus** that structure pages into a browsable hierarchy.
- **Templates** for creating consistent pages.
- **Tags**, **comments** and **relations/links** between pages and other TermX resources, so knowledge and terminology stay connected.
- **Full-text search** across pages and content.
- **Multilingual** content and human-friendly URLs.
- Isolation into [spaces](page:space), so different projects keep their own body of knowledge.

Content can be exported and published as a static site (see [static site generation](page:static-site-generation)) or, since release 3.3, as **PDF/HTML** for a page or a whole space.

See the [wiki](page:wiki) page for the full description of the platform and its capabilities.
